Cycle assumptions & interpretation

Core chart · Bitcoin's stepwise decline in block rewards is always available without a price feed.

Important · Alignment to halving dates is not the same as alignment to market peaks or bottoms. A three-year rise followed by a one-year decline is a hypothesis, not a rule.

Limitations · Missing prices are never linearly fabricated. Provider pricing and time zones can differ.
