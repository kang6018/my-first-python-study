Limitations · Missing prices are never linearly fabricated. Provider pricing and time zones can differ.

Local storage

Chart layouts, strategies, memos and journals are saved only in this browser's localStorage. They do not sync across devices and may disappear if browsing data is cleared.

Sources & technical limitations

TradingView is an externally hosted widget: the website cannot access or persist its internal drawings or streaming tick data. BTC history uses public provider data or CSV. Fear & Greed uses Alternative.me; funding and open interest come from Binance USDⓈ-M public APIs. Regional or browser restrictions may block requests.

Unconnected on-chain metrics, liquidations and global liquidity are not fabricated. Analysis cannot guarantee investment returns.
